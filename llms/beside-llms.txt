## What Beside Does
Beside is an AI-powered business phone system that answers every call, captures every lead, and books every appointment — 24/7. It replaces voicemail, manual intake, and missed calls with an AI receptionist that works like your best employee.

Core tagline: "Beside runs your business. You do your job."

Key capabilities:
- AI Receptionist: answers inbound calls 24/7, captures caller details (name, phone, address, service type, urgency), qualifies leads, and routes information to the right person
- Automatic Call Summaries: 93.9% of calls are summarized automatically with zero manual data entry
- Call Transcripts: every call is transcribed and searchable — for case files, dispute resolution, and accountability
- Real Business Phone Number: use your own number or get a new one; 55% of businesses keep their existing number and forward calls
- Team Phone Management: admin dashboard, shared inboxes, roles & permissions (owner / admin / member), add/remove seats
- Unlimited Calls & Texts: US and Canada included, with affordable international rates
- Call Forwarding & Business Hours: calls ring your team during business hours, route to AI after hours
- Mobile + Web App: primary interface is mobile-first, with a web app
- Outbound Calling: place outbound calls from the Beside app (AI receptionist handles inbound only)
- Languages Supported: English, Spanish, French, and Portuguese — for both calls and texts
- Spam Blocking: 550,000+ spam calls blocked across the platform

## How the AI Receptionist Works
1. Call comes in → AI answers immediately, no hold music
2. AI qualifies the caller → captures name, phone, service need, urgency, address, and any custom fields
3. Structured summary created → pushed to CRM, Slack, email, or dispatch tool automatically
4. Team follows up with full context → transcript and summary attached

93.9% of calls are summarized automatically. Average AI call duration is 2–4 minutes depending on complexity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I get started?
Setup takes about 10 minutes — download the app, configure your AI receptionist, and go live. Most users set up during the day and their AI receptionist handles the first real business call the next morning. The fastest are live same-day.

Do I have to change my phone number?
No. 55% of businesses on Beside keep their existing number and forward calls — your callers keep dialing the same number, nothing changes on their end. You can also port your number to Beside entirely.

What happens after hours or during peak times?
Your AI Receptionist picks up 24/7 by default — evenings, weekends, holidays, and during your busiest hours. You can set business hours so calls ring your team during the day and route to the AI outside those windows.

Can Beside make outbound calls?
Yes. You can place outbound calls from the Beside app. The AI Receptionist currently handles inbound calls only.

Is my data secure?
All data is encrypted in transit and stored on AWS servers in the U.S. Your recordings, transcripts, and summaries stay private unless you choose to share them.

What languages are supported?
English, Spanish, French, and Portuguese — for both calls and texts.

Does the AI Receptionist replace my team?
It works alongside them. The AI handles calls based on the scenarios you define, collects the right details, and logs everything. Anything that needs a human gets handed off so your team stays focused on higher-value work.

What does per-user pricing include?
Every user includes a business phone number, AI receptionist, automatic call summaries and transcripts, shared inbox access, integrations, and the mobile + Web app. No hidden fees for minutes or AI usage. Each user gets one phone line; additional lines are $4.99/month each.
